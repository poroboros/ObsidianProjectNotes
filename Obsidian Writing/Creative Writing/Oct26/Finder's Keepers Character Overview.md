

### Glint (Steven)
Glint (Steven) has been a crow for about a week and house already lost most of his human memories. He was Boyfriends with Harvey when they were human. I want it to be ambigious whether Glint realizes who Harvey is to him, but I still want romance between them to be implied. Glint is our perspective character. Glint has fully acclimated to the idea of being a bird, finding human things to be weird and scary. His knowlege of human things such as clothes is limited.

### Harvey (Ruffle)
Harvey is naturally very distressed, being in full panic from the transformation. Despite that he is still concerned about finding Steven. He does not want to stay with Glint until after the kernels wipe his memory.

For the scope of this story, this will be mostly unexplained. She is some kind Circe like witch who keeps the crows, perhaps for company.
