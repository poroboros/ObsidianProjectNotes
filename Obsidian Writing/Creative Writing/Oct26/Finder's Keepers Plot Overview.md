[[Finder's Keepers Character Overview]]
### Exposition
Glint is introduced, notices another bird near an unassembled scarecrow.  Glint finds that the other bird, named Harvey, is freaking out, claiming that he used to be a human. Harvey was looking for his (boy)friend Steven, who went missing after visiting the cornfield with his friends. 

### Conflict
Glint does not believe Harvey was a human, as that is not possible. Harvey becomes increasingly distraught at this. Suddenly Glint stops talking and says they need to leave. After a gunshot, Glint flees and Harvey follows, not before being wounded by a stray birdshot.

### Rising Action
Harvey comes to rest near one of Glint's stashes, where we see some of his items. Harvey passes out from the pain. Glint decides to go out at night to get some food from the corn field.

### Climax
Glint notices an old woman putting Harvey's old clothes onto the scarecrow. Glint cannot help himself and sneaks over to steal the heart necklace. The old woman turns around and Glint freezes uncontrollably. The old woman kneels down to pet Glint and hands him kernels to take back with him.

### Falling Action
Glint brings the kernels back to Harvey and feeds them to him. Harvey puts the necklace in his stash revealing the other heart necklace "H+S".

### Conclusion
Harvey wakes up, unable to remember his name. Glint tells him that he should pick a name and he picks Ruffle. Glint says he likes that and cuddles up to him to sleep.
