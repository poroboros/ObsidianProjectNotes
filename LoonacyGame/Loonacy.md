## Overview 
Loonacy is an online co-op survivor-like game in which you fight off hoards of villainous waterfowl who are attempting to steal your lake.

## Quick Facts

**Genre:** Action, survivor-like, some roguelike elements.
**Multiplayer:** 1-4~ish players. Online first, hopefully local co-op supporting.
**Aesthetic:** Pseudo 3-D top down, low-poly due to convenience.  PSX shaders maybe.
**Scope:** Small until proven, one map for now
**Architecture:** Component driven development, ideally should be reproducable.
